# About Clinisync

**Clinisync** is a secure, intelligent ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) tool designed specifically to clean, convert, and protect clinical Excel data. Developed with healthcare data governance and compliance in mind, Clinisync ensures sensitive information is transformed into anonymized, structured outputs.

### 🎯 Vision
To empower healthcare teams with seamless tools to convert unstructured Excel records into clean, analysis-ready datasets — without sacrificing privacy or accuracy.

### 🚀 Goals
- Simplify Excel uploads from various sources and structures.
- Automatically clean and remove PHI (e.g., address, contact).
- Normalize patient IDs across multi-sheet datasets.
- Convert DOB to accurate age calculations.
- Support bias-sensitive transformations and logic.
- Enable API-based automation for integration into broader clinical data systems.
