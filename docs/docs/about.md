# About Clinisync

**Clinisync** is a secure, intelligent ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) tool designed specifically to clean, convert, and protect clinical Excel data. The platform emphasizes healthcare data governance and compliance, transforming sensitive information into anonymized, structured datasets.

## Vision

Empower healthcare teams to convert unstructured Excel records into clean, analysis-ready datasets — without sacrificing privacy or accuracy.

## Goals

Clinisync aims to:

- Streamline Excel uploads from diverse sources and formats.
- Automatically eliminate PHI (personally identifiable health information) such as addresses and contact details.
- Standardize patient IDs across multi-sheet datasets.
- Transform dates of birth into precise age calculations.
- Incorporate bias-conscious transformations and business logic.
- Provide API-based automation for integration with broader clinical data systems.
