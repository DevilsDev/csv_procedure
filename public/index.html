Clinisync

Upload a clinical Excel workbook and get back de-identified, per-sheet CSVs ready for analysis.

What gets cleaned

These rules apply automatically to every sheet in your upload. The output never contains the original NHI, DOB, address, or contact details.

NHI → ID Replaced with sequential anonymized IDs (ID-001, ID-002, …) shared across sheets in the same upload.
DOB → Age Converted to whole-year age, clamped to 0..130. Invalid dates become empty.
Address / Contact Columns named exactly Address or Contact are dropped entirely.
Duplicates & blanks Exact-duplicate rows after transformation are removed. Empty rows and unnamed columns are skipped.
